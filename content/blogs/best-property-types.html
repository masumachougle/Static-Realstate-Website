Although Dubai continues to be one of the strongest rental markets in the major cities around the world, the year 2026 is a different scenario from the years of boom. The rental prices are steady, a huge number of new properties are entering the market, and the differences between some good and some average investments are expanding.

The market backdrop

As of August 2026, the average gross rental yield for residential properties in Dubai was recorded at 6.3%, with apartments achieving the highest yield of 6.7%, followed by townhouses and villas getting a yield of 5.1% and 4.5%, respectively. Yield is understood as annual rent divided by purchase price, before deductions. After the costs of service charges, maintenance, management and vacancy, net returns fall lower by approximately 1.5 to 2.5 percentage points, which means that when a 7 percent yield is achieved, around 4.5–5 percent net yield could be expected.

The issue is further compounded by softening in terms of Dubai rents. A report from CBRE shows that rents in Dubai dropped by 6.2% in Q2 2026 while prices stayed above the 2025 levels. Moody's reported that there will be over 150,000 new houses built in Dubai between 2025 and 2027, which accounts for a 20% increase in their number.

1. Studios and one-bedroom apartments

This yields the classic yield play. Studios and one-bedroom apartments in the middle market often yield 7% to 9% gross, while large three-bedroom apartments and villas yield only 4% to 6%. The smaller units benefit since those earn the highest rents per square foot, meaning prices don't rise uniformly.

The problem is the oversupply. Some areas, such as Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), offer high yields, but Property Finder predictions list Dubai South, JVC, and Business Bay as oversupplied areas with weak or mixed rental growth. Therefore, it is important to check for properties where the yield is higher than in surrounding towers, since high yield can be paled by low quality, bad access to the metro, or high service charges in the particular area.

2. Mid-market apartments in established communities

While a steady demand is more critical than a liquid maximum yield, places such as Dubai Marina, Business Bay, and Dubai Hills possess yields between 5.5 and 7%, respectively, with greater appreciation and liquidity. Data projected by Property Finder shows Dubai Marina and Dubai Hills Estate as locations that face growth that is either mild or steady due to high demand.

3. Holiday homes (short-term rentals)

In theory, short-term rentals perform better than yearly leases. According to one operator, optimal yearly leases yield 5–7% while upgraded short-term rental homes earn 8%–14%. However, the difference is negligible in practice. According to the same operator, the actual net profit margin is in the 25%–50% range; moreover, that city makes an average of 69% of revenue per year, with 50–60% in summer and a 30–45% drop in nightly rates.

Three practical points:

  • Licensing is essential. For rentals that are less than six months, you are required to acquire a holiday home permit from the Department of Economy and Tourism (previously known as DTCM). If operating without proper licensing, one can incur penalties ranging between AED 5,000 and AED 100,000.
  • You need to ascertain the building first. Certain buildings may prevent short-term rentals as per the rules laid down by the owners' association.
  • It requires active efforts. In fact, operating a holiday home is like managing a mini business in case you do not hire a licensed operator.

Due to its substantial dependence on tourism, this business sector suffers from various crises as well. According to CBRE, the economy of the UAE is going to face a slight contraction of 0.04% in 2026 due to disruptions in trade, tourism, and aviation.

4. Villas and townhouses

Villas have lower returns of about 4.5%, but their role is different. According to Property Finder, villa communities and those located on the waterfront are more resilient inherently due to a limited number of new properties, while villa rents increased even though apartments on the market have gone down. Total returns suggest that in the case of long-term housing investors, villas are preferred because they are located in reputable communities.

5. A niche worth knowing: industrial and logistics

The industrial and logistics market sector is operating strongly, with rental rates growing in Dubai Industrial City, Dubai Investment Park, and National Industries Park. The sector usually requires a solid budget and specialized expertise, which is why it is more appealing to experienced investors rather than newcomers.

What "passive" really means

The costs of service charges, maintenance, management fees, insurance, and property vacancy will erode the return on an investment. Rental income can be tax-exempt in many cases, but a company structure may incur corporate taxes at a rate of 9% on the net rental income above AED 375,000, according to one advisory company. Investors should evaluate if a purchase makes sense even if there is no rental growth and you must be able to sell the property in two years.

The bottom line

When searching for maximizing return on each dirham spent, the focus must be on the studios and one-bedroom apartments in good buildings. It is necessary to estimate the local supply situation ahead of placing trust in the published yield figures. To achieve a proper balance, choose mature mid-market community areas. For potentially higher returns, consider investing in the holiday homes, subject to ensuring the required licenses and complying with the regulations first. To achieve the growth of assets over time, choose villas situated in limited-supply locations as a complementary type of investment.

Please note that the yield and projections differ by source and change rather quickly. Most published data comes from real estate agencies and brokers wishing to sell, so it is recommended to check it versus the latest numbers available from the Dubai Land Department and talk to an authorized professional prior to the purchase.
